How to stop doomscrolling

Doomscrolling rarely feels like a choice. You open an app for one thing, and twenty minutes later you are still in the feed. Willpower usually loses against an endless feed, so the trick is to put a small pause back between you and the next swipe.

Five steps that actually help

  1. Name your trigger apps. Usually it’s two or three: social media, short videos, maybe a game.
  2. Give them a budget instead of a ban. Total bans tend to get switched off within days.
  3. Decide how many minutes you want before you open the app.
  4. Make the end visible. A clear stop works better than “I’ll put it down soon”.
  5. Have something ready instead: a walk, a workout, a chapter of a book.

Frequently asked questions

What is doomscrolling?

Endless scrolling through feeds, often bad news or short videos, long after it stopped being useful or fun.

Is it better to block social media completely?

For some people it is. Many find a fixed budget easier to keep than a total ban, because it doesn’t feel like a punishment.

Does ScrollVolt delete or hide my apps?

No. Your apps stay on your phone. They are only shielded when your battery is empty or a focus session is running.
