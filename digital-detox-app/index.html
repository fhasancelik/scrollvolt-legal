A digital detox app for iPhone that doesn’t make you quit

A digital detox doesn’t have to mean a weekend with your phone in a drawer. For most people the detox that lasts is a daily one: less mindless scrolling, more time for everything else.

Plan a detox you can keep

  1. Pick the few apps that eat most of your time and leave the useful ones alone.
  2. Set a daily budget instead of a full ban.
  3. Plan phone-free blocks for workouts, study or deep work.
  4. Reduce gradually, a few minutes each day.
  5. Look at your week so you can see progress.

How ScrollVolt helps

ScrollVolt covers each of these steps. The apps you choose share a scroll battery that drains while you use them and recharges on its own over the day. You decide how many minutes to unlock by dragging the battery, and at zero a calm shield screen replaces the app. Focus sessions keep distracting apps locked while you work out or study, and charge the battery twice as fast. The Half Challenge lowers your daily limit by 5, 10 or 15 minutes a day, and the weekly trend shows whether it is working.

It is private, too: ScrollVolt is built on Apple’s Screen Time frameworks and everything runs on your iPhone, with no account, no tracking and no ads.

Frequently asked questions

What is a digital detox?

A deliberate break from screens, or from specific apps, to reset your habits. It can be a whole day offline or a smaller daily limit.

Do I need to block my whole phone?

No. Day to day, ScrollVolt only limits the apps you pick. Everything else works as usual.

Which languages is ScrollVolt available in?

The app is available in English, German, Spanish and Turkish.
