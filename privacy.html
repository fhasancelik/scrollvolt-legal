Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

ScrollVolt ("the app") is built so that your data stays on your device. We do not run servers that receive your data, we do not have user accounts, and we do not sell, share or track you.

Screen Time data

The app uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings and DeviceActivity) to block and unblock the apps, categories and websites you choose. Apple only gives the app anonymous tokens for your selections — the app cannot see which apps they are, and your app usage is processed entirely on your device. This data is never transmitted off your device.

Data stored on your device

Your name (if you enter one), your goals, battery settings, session history and app preferences are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete my data, removes it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No push notification service is used.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from them.

Feedback

If you choose to send feedback, it is submitted through the support page you open in your browser and contains only what you write.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
