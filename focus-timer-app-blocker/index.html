A focus timer that blocks apps while you study

A study timer only works if your phone stays quiet. ScrollVolt’s focus sessions combine a timer with an app blocker, so distracting apps stay locked until the session is over.

How to start a focus session

  1. Open ScrollVolt and tap Focus.
  2. Pick what you are doing: study, gym, work, reading or something else.
  3. Optional: choose how long to lock it for. A locked session can’t be ended early.
  4. Add allowed apps you still need, like a dictionary or your calendar.
  5. Tap Start Session and put the phone face down.

Why it works for studying

The hardest part of studying is the first ten minutes, when every notification looks more interesting than your notes. During a focus session your distracting apps stay locked, so checking “just quickly” is off the table. As a reward, your scroll battery charges twice as fast while you focus: a solid study block earns you guilt-free scrolling afterwards. Try blocks of 25 to 50 minutes with short breaks in between. Your history keeps a log of every focus session you finish.

Frequently asked questions

Can I end a focus session early?

If you set a lock time, no. That is the point: the session holds until the time is up. Without a lock time, you can end it yourself.

Which apps are blocked during a session?

Your distracting apps stay locked for the whole session. Apps you mark as allowed stay available.

Does it work for workouts too?

Yes. Sessions also work for the gym, deep work and reading, and every one charges your battery 2x faster.
